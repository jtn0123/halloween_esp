Halloween Castle

Set up a new castle, or put a castle's software back the way it came.

Before you start

  • Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a Windows PC or a Mac. Safari and Firefox cannot do this.
  • A USB-C cable that carries data (the one that came with the castle does). Some phone-charging cables only carry power.
  • Your home Wi-Fi name and password.

Steps

  1. Plug the castle into the computer with the USB cable.
  2. Click Connect to the castle below.
  3. A small window lists devices. Pick the one that appeared when you plugged the castle in, then click Connect.
  4. Choose Install. If it asks whether to erase the castle, say yes for a fresh start — your songs on the memory card are not touched.
  5. Wait about two minutes. Keep the cable plugged in until it says it is done.
  6. When it asks, choose your Wi-Fi network and type its password. The castle joins your Wi-Fi and is ready.
This browser can't talk to the castle. Open this page in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a computer. This page has to be opened over https:// to reach the castle.

If the computer can't find the castle

  • Try a different USB cable or a different USB port — this fixes it most often.
  • Still nothing? On the castle's circuit board, hold the button marked BOOT, tap the button marked RESET, then let go of BOOT. Click Connect to the castle again.
  • On Windows 10 and 11 no driver is needed. If the device list stays empty, unplug the castle, wait five seconds and plug it back in.

Already set up?

You don't need this page to update the castle — the Castle app does that over Wi-Fi. This page is for a new castle, a new Wi-Fi network, or a castle that stopped starting up.
