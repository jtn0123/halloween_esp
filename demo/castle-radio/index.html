Setup & startup

On your Mac, run this once in the Halloween Castle project folder:

./tools/install_castle_tools.sh

Then double-click Open Castle Studio.command in that folder. It starts your tools. Open the castle website and choose Connect Mac tools. Keep the small connection window open too. Keep its Terminal window open while you work.

On the castle website, Connect Mac tools enables importing here. Allow the small connection window to open and keep it open.

Startup never installs or upgrades anything. If a check fails, run the installer again, then restart Castle Studio.
