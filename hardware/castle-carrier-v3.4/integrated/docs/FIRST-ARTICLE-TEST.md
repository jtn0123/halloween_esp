# Integrated v3.4 first-board acceptance

Status: NOT RUN. This procedure prepares physical tests; software/CAD checks cannot supply measured results. Record PCB and firmware hashes, supply model, speaker impedance, cable length, SD card, LED count and test duration for each board. The existing printed v3.3a keeps its mono firmware and is excluded from this stereo procedure.

1. **Inspect before power.** Check component MPNs, pin 1/polarities, R29 100k/R30 374k, cartridge installation, filled/capped exposed-pad joints and supplier X-ray. Check for solder bridges and an unpowered hard short. Keep each bridged speaker connected only between its own +/- terminals; neither is GND.
2. **Start with barrel power.** Begin at low current limit, with LED loads disconnected and audio stopped. Raise the limit as startup needs; record input current and 5 V/3.3 V waveforms. The intended GST25A05-P1J is 5 V, 4 A (20 W output), with +/-5% voltage tolerance. A fuse is supplementary protection and does not guarantee a 4 A cutoff. Verify 3.3 V nominal regulation and all startup/transient excursions remain inside the ESP32 module's 3.0-3.6 V range.
3. **Check source switching and USB.** Test USB-only idle/service use with LED loads disconnected; record insertion current and VBUS dip. Do not assume a PC's USB port supports the 4 A barrel load. Test both USB-C orientations, repeated enumeration, BOOT/RESET, flashing/recovery and J10 UART at 3.3 V logic. J10 pin 1 is 5 V. With each supply absent in turn, measure backfeed at the absent input; also test both connected.
4. **Verify firmware and storage.** Use the Integrated N16R8 overlay and private credentials. Confirm actual detected 16 MB flash and 8 MB PSRAM in logs, GPIO39/40/41 SD operations, a known test-file read/checksum, safe card removal/error handling, OTA and rollback/recovery. Save serial logs, not credentials.
5. **Prove stereo.** Copy the supplied MP3 to `tests/channel-identification.mp3` on SD and use the existing `play_sd` path. At low volume: 0-2 s LEFT-only 440 Hz, 3-5 s RIGHT-only 660 Hz, 6-8 s both 440 Hz; intervening periods are silent. Then test mono MP3 duplicated to both speakers and representative MP3/WAV/Opus rates through the 44.1 kHz resampler. Record separation, audible glitches, clock/rail captures and speaker load. The fixture was decoded and checked on the computer, not played on this PCB.
6. **Combine realistic maximum loads.** Run the intended lights, stereo, SD streaming and Wi-Fi together from the 4 A barrel supply for at least 30 minutes. Keep total current within its rating; determine the usable LED/volume limit from measurements. Capture load steps and hot restart. AP63203 minimum input is 3.8 V: use >=4.0 V at its VIN under load as a prototype margin target, not a manufacturer-wide board rating. Record minimum buck input, minimum/maximum 3.3 V, resets, audio/SD errors and fuse/MOSFET/buck/module/amplifier/connector temperatures; compare component temperatures to their datasheet ratings. An earlier illustrative hot 4 A model was near/below the buck minimum, so this is an open acceptance gate.
7. **Check the physical installation.** The PCB is 100 x 80 mm and satisfies the only provided width limit. Use the dimensioned hole coordinates, including the 0.4 mm top-hole offset. Check terminal screwdriver/wire access, cable boots, SD travel, underside lead length, height and RF range with the chosen mounting arrangement. The module body sits within the PCB; the extra 14.75 mm RF keepout is clearance, not a protruding body. No enclosure model was supplied.

Use `first-article-results.csv` to record actual measurements and PASS/FAIL decisions. Keep NOT RUN until measured. Supplier acceptance permits prototype fabrication; successful powered tests are a separate qualification step.
