How faithful this is. The effect maths, per-pixel seeds and cue timings are the firmware's own (ported line for line from castle_effects.h and generated from scenes.yaml), and the rendered audio is byte-identical to what ships in flash. What the screen cannot promise is pixel gamma: WS2812-family LEDs diverge from sRGB hardest at exactly the low brightness where flicker lives, so budget one retune pass when the jewels arrive.

    
Why thunder fires before the flash. Light beats sound to an observer in the real world, so leading with audio is physically right — and it conveniently swallows the playback trigger latency.
